1664 Blanc x Boulebar

Brand partnership, summer events and content

The idea

A beer brand and a pétanque bar both wanted people to come back all summer. I pitched them a Sunday pétanque series with 1664 Blanc built into the day.

What I did

Developed and pitched the partnership, ran five Sunday events with a host, a DJ and prizes, and shot all the photo and video.

The result

Attendance grew 257% over the summer. Featured on The Jungle List, and the concept came back the following summer.
