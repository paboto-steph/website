04 / How I work

From a hunch to a full room

Every project gets its own plan. This is roughly how it goes.

  1. 01

    Define

    What's the situation, the challenge or the goal? Research, data and human insight, plus a few calls to people who know.

  2. 02

    Find the hook

    What would make people feel they belong? I look at what works for similar brands and in other industries.

  3. 03

    Make it physical

    An experience or community that fits what people are already talking about. You get a deck with a rollout plan.

  4. 04

    Produce

    Partners, people, run-of-show and a camera. I'm there on the day.

  5. 05

    Launch and learn

    We go live, then look at what the numbers say and what to do next.
