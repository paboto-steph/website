French Summer Moments

Client
1664 Blanc x Boulebar
Sector
Food & drink, Hospitality
Services
Partnership, activation, photo and video
Result
+257% event attendance

Challenge

A French beer and a pétanque bar with the same easy-going crowd, and a whole Copenhagen summer to fill.

Idea

Make a small French ritual out of it. Pétanque every Sunday, a cold 1664 Blanc, and a good reason to come back next week.

What I did

  • Developed and pitched the partnership from scratch
  • Ran five Sunday pétanque events
  • Shot all photo and video
  • All social, all organic

Result

Event attendance grew 257% over the summer, with no paid media. Featured on The Jungle List, and the series continues.
