About

Stephanie Pabotoy. Creative consultant and photographer in Copenhagen, working as PABOTO.

Hi, I'm Steph. I bring good people together around food, drink and culture, and I photograph what happens.

Over the last ten years I've done the ABC of branding, on both the client and the agency side. It's given me a 360-degree view of how a concept needs to be executed to actually work for a brand.

I'm at my best seeing where partners can meet and both benefit, then knocking on the right doors to make it happen. Casting for film, pitching branded podcasts, pop-ups and partnerships are some of my favourite jobs. And I love documenting the process: people living their craft.

How I work

  1. The ideaWhat makes people want to show up, and why it fits your brand.
  2. The pitch and the deckA clear story your team, partners and sponsors say yes to.
  3. The right peopleCasting, partners, venues and crew, matched so everyone benefits.
  4. The momentThe day itself, offline and real.
  5. Photo and social firstI shoot it myself, made for the feed, so the moment travels.
