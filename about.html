About

Stephanie Pabotoy. Creative consultant and photographer in Copenhagen, working as PABOTO.

Hi, I'm Steph. I bring good people together around food, drink and culture, and I photograph what happens.

Over the last ten years I've done the ABC of branding, on both the client and the agency side. It's given me a 360-degree view of how a concept needs to be executed to actually work for a brand.

I'm at my best seeing where partners can meet and both benefit, then knocking on the right doors to make it happen. From the idea and the pitch, to the right people, the day itself and the photos. And I love documenting the process: people living their craft.
